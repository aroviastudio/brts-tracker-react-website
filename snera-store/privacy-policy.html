At Snera Store, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. When you browse or purchase from our website, we collect necessary personal details including your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment confirmation details.

Your information is used strictly to process orders, fulfill delivery of handcrafted sarees, send order status notifications, respond to customer service inquiries, and ensure secure transaction processing.

We do not store your complete credit card, debit card, or UPI PIN credentials on our servers. All online transactions are securely encrypted and processed directly through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (Razorpay). We never sell or lease your personal data to third parties.

We use session cookies to keep track of your shopping cart contents and remember your preferences for a seamless checkout experience.
