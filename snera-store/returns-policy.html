Returns & Refund Policy

Snera Store — 7-Day Hassle-Free Return Guarantee

1. 7-Day Return Window

At Snera Store, we want you to love your handcrafted saree. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may initiate a return or exchange request within 7 days of delivery.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a return:

  • The saree must be unused, unwashed, unworn, and in its original condition with all brand tags intact.
  • The original saree box and packaging must be included.

3. Order Cancellation & Refunds

You may cancel an order directly from your profile dashboard while the status is pending or paid (before shipment). For prepaid orders, online refunds are processed back to your original payment method (bank account, UPI, card) within 5 to 7 business days.

4. How to Request a Return

To request a return, contact our customer support team via email at support@snerastore.com or WhatsApp us with your order number.
