Shipping & Delivery Policy

Snera Store — Fast & Secure Nationwide Shipping

1. Dispatch & Processing Time

All orders placed at Snera Store are quality-checked, carefully packaged, and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation.

2. Delivery Timeframes

  • Metro Cities in India: 3 to 5 business days.
  • Rest of India: 5 to 7 business days.

3. Shipping Charges

We offer FREE Shipping across India for orders above ₹999 (or on products flagged with free delivery). For orders below ₹999, a flat shipping fee of ₹100 applies.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email notification containing your courier tracking number and real-time tracking link.
