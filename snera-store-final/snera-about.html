Our Genesis

Woven by Passion, Bound by Heritage

Founded in Gujarat, Snera Store emerged from a reverent admiration for India's indigenous textile heritage. For generations, master weavers have translated poetic folklore into opulent silk tapestries using wooden pit looms, real zari, and meticulous jacquard shuttles.

In a world dominated by rapid powerloom replication, we champion slow fashion. By eliminating intermediaries and working hand-in-hand with traditional artisan clusters in Varanasi, Kanchipuram, Chanderi, and Bengal, we ensure fair compensation while offering you unmatched handloom purity.
